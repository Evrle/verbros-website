MUSEAI Smart Mannequin

A mannequin that restyles itself for every passer-by.

MUSE is an on-device computer-vision mannequin built on the NVIDIA Jetson Nano Developer Kit. It detects shoppers as they approach, analyses attributes and demographics at the edge, and changes the outfits and cross-sell suggestions it shows to suit each one. All inference happens on the device.

Window displays that work for everyone who walks past.

A traditional mannequin shows one look to everyone, all day, until someone changes it by hand. It can't tell who is looking, and nobody knows whether it brought a single person into the store.

MUSE gives the display eyes and timing. A discreet camera and a Jetson Nano detect each passer-by, estimate attributes such as age band and gender, and pick the most relevant look and add-ons from your range to show on the screen beside the mannequin. It also counts how many people stopped and which looks held their attention.

How MUSE works

From the moment someone walks into view to the moment the display changes, every step runs on the device.

  1. Spot

    A camera in the mannequin or display detects shoppers approaching and tracks them while they are in view.

  2. Read the moment

    On-device models estimate attributes and demographics, such as age band, gender and group size, on the Jetson Nano.

  3. Pick the look

    Your merchandising rules and a relevance model choose the outfit and cross-sell items most likely to interest that shopper.

  4. Show and measure

    The display updates to the chosen look and records anonymous counts: passers-by, stops, attention time and which looks performed.

Features

Everything a visual merchandiser would do for each shopper, if they could stand in the window all day.

  • Shopper detection

    Detects and tracks people approaching the display in real time, on the device.

  • Attribute and demographic analysis

    Estimates age band, gender and group size to choose relevant content.

  • Dynamic outfits

    Shows the look most relevant to each passer-by, drawn from your current range.

  • Cross-sell suggestions

    Pairs every look with add-ons such as footwear and accessories, and shows where to find them in store.

  • Attention measurement

    Counts passers-by, how many stopped and how long they looked.

  • Display performance

    See which looks drew attention by hour and day, and refine your visual merchandising.

  • On-device privacy

    All inference runs on the Jetson Nano. No video or images leave the mannequin.

  • Merchandising control

    Your team sets the looks, rules and schedules. MUSE chooses within the limits you define.

  • Works offline

    Runs without internet, and connects only to sync content and anonymous counts.

Questions about MUSE

Can't find your answer here? Ask us directly.

Does MUSE record shoppers?

No. Video is analysed in memory on the Jetson Nano and discarded. Only anonymous counts, such as passers-by and stops, are kept.

Does the mannequin change clothes by itself?

The mannequin wears a physical outfit. MUSE changes what the display beside it shows: alternative looks, colours and add-ons chosen for each passer-by.

Can we control which looks are shown?

Yes. Your team sets the looks, product rules and schedules. MUSE chooses within those limits for each shopper.

What does it need on site?

Power, a mounting point for the camera and display, and Wi-Fi or Ethernet for content sync.

Do MUSE and IRIS work together?

They complement each other. MUSE draws shoppers to a look in the window, and IRIS helps them try it and find what pairs with it at the mirror.
