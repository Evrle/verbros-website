Your supply chain data lives in five places. Your questions shouldn't have to.

Planners and managers spend hours moving between ERP screens, warehouse reports, transport portals, production logs and spreadsheets to answer simple questions. By the time the answer has been pieced together in Excel, the situation has changed.

ATLAS connects to those systems and builds one model of your supply chain: materials, stock, orders, shipments, production and suppliers. You ask in plain language. A team of AI agents works out which systems to query, combines the results, explains the answer and, when you ask, drafts the action for you to approve.

How ATLAS works

ATLAS follows the same loop for every request: connect, unify, reason, then act only with approval.

  1. Connect

    Connectors read from your ERP (built and tested on an SAP sandbox), WMS, TMS, MES and spreadsheets, with read-only access by default.

  2. Unify

    A semantic layer maps everything to shared business entities: SKUs, plants, warehouses, purchase and sales orders, shipments and work orders.

  3. Ask

    A planning agent breaks your question into steps, data agents query each system, and an analyst agent combines and explains the result.

  4. Act with approval

    For tasks like transfers, PO follow-ups or reschedules, ATLAS drafts the action and waits for an authorised person to approve it.

  5. Watch

    Alerts you write in plain language run on a schedule and notify you the moment a condition is met.

Automation you can trust with real data

ATLAS is designed so that AI can help run the supply chain without anyone losing control of it.

Read-only by default

Connectors start with read access. Write access is granted per action type, only when you choose.

Role-based permissions

People see and act only on the data their role allows, in ATLAS as in your source systems.

Approval thresholds

Set limits by value, quantity or action type. Anything above them always needs a person.

Show the working

Answers and proposed actions show the queries, records and assumptions behind them.

Full audit trail

Questions, drafts, approvals and executed actions are all logged for review.

Policy checks

Proposed actions are checked against business rules, like minimum stock or approved suppliers, before they reach you.

Questions about ATLAS

Can't find your answer here? Ask us directly.

Does ATLAS replace our ERP or dashboards?

No. ATLAS sits on top of your existing systems and reads from them. It removes the need to build a new report or dashboard for every question.

Which systems does it work with?

ATLAS has been built and tested on an SAP sandbox and works with WMS, TMS and MES data and with Excel and CSV files. We confirm connectors for your specific systems during scoping.

Can ATLAS change data in our systems?

Only if you allow it. Connectors are read-only by default. When write access is enabled for an action type, every change is drafted for a person to approve and is logged.

How are alerts set up?

You write the condition in plain language, such as “tell me when raw material cover at Plant 2 drops below five days”. ATLAS turns it into a scheduled check and notifies you when it is met.

How do we know an answer is right?

Each answer shows which systems and records were used and how the result was calculated, so it can be checked.
