Four AI products, each built to do one job very well.

ARGUS measures what happens in your stores. IRIS and MUSE personalise the shop floor. ATLAS gives your supply chain a voice. Here is what each one does, where it runs and who it is for.

edge + cloud

ARGUS

Video Analytics + LLM Insights

Every CCTV camera you own, turned into an analyst.

ARGUS ingests RTSP and CCTV feeds at the edge and turns them into KPIs: age and gender mix, crowd count, dwell, queues, occupancy, visits and cross-store comparisons. An LLM layer answers questions and runs alerts you write in plain language.

Runs on
Edge device on site + cloud
Input
RTSP / CCTV / NVR streams
Output
KPIs, answers, alerts
Best for
Retail chains, malls, branches

IRIS

AI Smart Mirror

A mirror that knows what suits each shopper.

IRIS runs face and attribute detection and virtual try-on on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano inside the mirror, then recommends products from your catalogue. Every frame stays on the device.

Runs on
NVIDIA Jetson Nano, on-device
Input
Camera facing the shopper
Output
Try-on and recommendations
Best for
Fashion, eyewear, beauty

MUSE

AI Smart Mannequin

A mannequin that restyles itself for every passer-by.

MUSE detects shoppers as they approach, estimates attributes and demographics at the edge and shows the outfits and cross-sell suggestions most relevant to each one. It also measures who stopped and which looks worked.

Runs on
NVIDIA Jetson Nano, on-device
Input
Camera at the display
Output
Dynamic looks, cross-sell, counts
Best for
Apparel brands, store windows

agentic AI

ATLAS

Supply Chain Intelligent Assistant (SCIA)

Ask your supply chain anything. Approve what it suggests.

ATLAS, our Supply Chain Intelligent Assistant (SCIA), unifies ERP, WMS, TMS and MES data with your spreadsheets in one chat. Agents answer questions, run prompt-driven workflows and draft actions for a person to approve.

Connects to
SAP ERP, WMS, TMS, MES, sheets
Interface
One chat window
Output
Answers, workflows, alerts
Best for
Supply chain and operations
