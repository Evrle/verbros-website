We build AI that works where your business happens.

Verbros Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a next-generation AI company based in Lucknow. We create AI products that turn raw data into actionable insight, automate routine work and make digital and in-store experiences smarter, for businesses and the customers they serve.

An AI product company

Verbros designs, builds and deploys its own AI products. We work across computer vision, edge AI, large language models and agentic automation, and we take each product from prototype to something a store manager, planner or shopper can use every day.

We serve both B2B and B2C sectors. For enterprises, we deliver AI-driven analytics, predictive maintenance and intelligent process automation that streamline operations, optimise supply chains and reduce costs. For consumers, we build experiences like smart mirrors and mannequins that make shopping personal without compromising privacy.

Our aim is simple: help businesses grow smarter and faster with AI that is practical, adaptable and responsible.

What we're good at

Six disciplines sit behind every Verbros product, and behind the custom solutions we build for partners.

Computer vision

Detection, tracking, attribute estimation and try-on, tuned for real stores and real cameras.

Edge AI

Models optimised with NVIDIA TensorRT to run in real time on Jetson devices and on-site GPUs.

LLMs and insights

Language models that turn metrics and records into answers, explanations and summaries.

Agentic AI

Agents that plan, query systems and draft actions inside guardrails, with people approving.

Predictive analytics

Forecasting and predictive maintenance from equipment, production and demand data.

Data engineering

Connectors and data models that bring ERP, WMS, TMS, MES and spreadsheets together.

How we work with partners

We prove value on a small scale first, then grow it. Each stage has a clear outcome before the next one starts.

  1. Discover

    We start with the decision or task you want to improve, and the cameras, devices and systems you already have.

  2. Prototype

    We fit one of our products, or build a focused prototype, on your data or a representative sample.

  3. Pilot

    We run it in one store, site or team, measure results against your baseline and tune it.

  4. Scale

    We roll it out across locations and teams, with monitoring, support and continuous improvement.

Principles we build on

The same four ideas shape every product we make.

  • Privacy by architecture

    Vision runs on the device or on site. IRIS and MUSE never send video anywhere, and ARGUS sends only anonymous metrics to the cloud.

  • Prompts over dashboards

    Ask in plain language and set alerts with a sentence, instead of learning yet another dashboard.

  • Works with what you have

    Existing CCTV over RTSP, SAP and other ERPs, warehouse and transport systems, and your spreadsheets.

  • People stay in charge

    Automation runs inside guardrails, and anything that changes business data waits for a person to approve it.
